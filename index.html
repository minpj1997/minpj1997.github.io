I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Singapore Management University, with expected completion in June 2027.

My primary field is econometrics. My research focuses on reliable inference with many instruments and covariates, clustered data, and high-dimensional regression models. I also have interests in high-dimensional statistics and causal inference.
