Welcome.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Singapore Management University, with expected completion in June 2027.

My primary field is econometrics. I develop econometric methods for reliable inference in high-dimensional regressions. I also have interests in high-dimensional statistics, statistical machine learning, and causal inference.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.
